
# Financial Data Validation

## Objective
Verify selected financial metrics obtained using
yfinance against official company disclosures.

## FY2024 Verification

| Company | Metric | Dashboard | Official |
|---|---|---|---|
| TCS | Revenue (INR crore) | 240,893 | 240,893 |
| TCS | Net income attributable to shareholders (INR crore) | 45,908 | 45,908 |
| Infosys | Revenue (USD billion) | 18.56 | 18.562 |
| Infosys | Net profit (USD billion) | 3.17 | 3.169 |

The displayed values agree within rounding precision.

## Sources
- TCS FY2024 consolidated IFRS financial statements:
  https://www.tcs.com/content/dam/tcs/investor-relations/financial-statements/2023-24/q4/IFRS/Extracts%20from%20Consolidated%2C%20Unaudited%20-%20INR.pdf
- Infosys consolidated IFRS financials:
  https://www.infosys.com/investors/reports-filings/financials/financials-ifrs.html

## Limitations
- Verification covers selected FY2024 metrics only.
- Reporting currencies differ between companies.
- Profit definitions may differ, especially regarding
  non-controlling interests.
- Financial statements must use comparable periods
  and accounting bases.
- Yahoo Finance data may be revised or incomplete.
